Features of SPC 6.5mm Flooring

The SPC 6.5mm range pairs a slimmer profile with the same click-lock convenience and realistic wood-grain finish. Six oak colours — from soft creams and greys to warm honey tones — make it easy to find the right look for your home.

  • Large-format 1524 × 228mm planks in a slimmer 6.5mm profile
  • Välinge click-lock system for fast floating installation
  • Realistic wood-grain emboss for an authentic timber feel
  • UV-coated surface
  • Micro bevel edge for a defined plank appearance
  • Six oak colours: HYB-6501 to HYB-6506

Six oak colourways, each shown as a surface swatch and an installed room scene

Sunlit Oak

Frosted Oak
A cool, mid-grey oak with soft weathered grain. Frosted Oak delivers a calm, contemporary look that pairs beautifully with white walls and charcoal accents.
